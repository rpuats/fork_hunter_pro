Installation

  1. Show your browser's bookmarks bar
  2. Drag the "Ghost Overlay" button above to the bookmarks bar
  3. Open any bookmaker's website
  4. Click the Ghost bookmark to activate
  5. Use keyboard shortcuts for quick actions
